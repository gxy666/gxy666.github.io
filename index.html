Xiangyu Gao

Ph.D. Candidate · Institute for Network Sciences and Cyberspace, Tsinghua University

I am a Ph.D. candidate at Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Ke Xu. My research focuses on programmable network traffic analysis and network security, including in-network DNN inference, congestion control for datacenter networks, and FPGA-enhanced programmable switches. I have completed my Ph.D. proposal defense. I also serve as the class assistant for senior Ph.D. students at the institute. Published at NSDI, SIGCOMM, IEEE ToN, and other top venues. Expected graduation: June 2027.
